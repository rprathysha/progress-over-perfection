Pre-Dental: Preparing for a Career in Dentistry

A look at what the Pre-Dental path involves — and the student leading it within Progress Over Perfection.

What is Pre-Dental?

Pre-Dental is the pathway for students preparing for dental school and a future career as a dentist (DDS or DMD). Like other pre-health tracks, it combines rigorous science coursework with the DAT, shadowing hours in dental offices, and hands-on experience that builds both clinical skill and confidence.

The workload can be just as demanding as it is rewarding, which is why this track has a place inside P.O.P. Staying grounded through the process means building habits that protect your well-being alongside your goals.

Coursework & DAT

A rigorous science course sequence paired with DAT preparation, all while maintaining a competitive GPA.

Shadowing & Clinical Hours

Time spent in dental offices and clinical settings that builds the hands-on experience dental schools look for.

Pre-Dental Chair

Dheeran Indiran

Senior Biology Major Psychology & Entrepreneurship Minors Aspiring Dentist

Dheeran is a senior Biology major on the Pre-Dental track, with minors in Psychology and Entrepreneurship. He aspires to one day own and operate his own private dental practice. Dheeran joined Progress Over Perfection (P.O.P.) because he is passionate about spreading mental health awareness among students pursuing careers in the pre-health fields. Having experienced the demanding nature of the pre-health curriculum, he understands how overwhelming the workload can become and how easily students can neglect their mental health and overall well-being while striving to meet academic and professional goals. Through his involvement with P.O.P., Dheeran hopes to help fellow pre-health students prioritize their mental health, maintain a healthy balance, and feel supported throughout their journey toward careers in healthcare.
