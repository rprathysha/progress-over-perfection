Meet Our Leadership

The Team Behind Progress Over Perfection

Meet the co-founders and officers building a community where pre-health students can prioritize their well-being alongside their goals.

Co-Founder & Co-President

Prathysha Ravi

Biological Sciences Health Promotion, Education & Behavior Minor Women’s & Gender Studies Minor Pre-Med

Prathysha Ravi is a pre-med student majoring in Biological Sciences with minors in Health Promotion, Education and Behavior and Women’s and Gender Studies. She is the co-founder of Progress Over Perfection (P.O.P.), an organization she created with Zamam Lone to address the academic stress and pressure that have become normalized among students pursuing careers in healthcare. What began as a HOSA mental health promotion project for SLC and ILC grew into a campus organization dedicated to providing pre-health students with resources, support, and a community where they can prioritize their well-being alongside their academic and professional goals. As a pre-med student, Prathysha understands the pressure that comes with rigorous coursework and preparing for a career in medicine. She manages her stress by creating structure in her day, setting aside time for academics while intentionally making space to rest and recharge. She believes in recognizing progress rather than focusing solely on what remains unfinished, and sees mistakes and setbacks as opportunities to grow. Through P.O.P., Prathysha hopes students realize they do not have to navigate the pre-health journey alone. She wants the organization to help students build supportive connections, access valuable resources, develop healthy strategies for managing stress, and ultimately feel confident and proud of their progress without sacrificing their mental well-being.

Co-Founder & Co-President

Zamam Lone

Biological Sciences Pre-Med

Zamam Lone is a pre-med student majoring in Biological Sciences and is one of the co-founders of Progress Over Perfection (P.O.P.). One of the main ways Zamam prioritizes his health is by taking care of his physical well-being. He believes that having a strong body can contribute to having a strong mind and enjoys maintaining routines and achieving small wins throughout the day. According to the NIH, exercise can reduce stress, ease anxiety and depression, and improve overall mood. In addition to physical activity, Zamam finds that talking to others whenever he is stressed or worried about something has been extremely beneficial. He believes that communication is one of the best traits a person can have and encourages others to reach out rather than keep their struggles to themselves. Zamam and fellow co-founder Prathysha started P.O.P. as a HOSA mental health promotion project for SLC and ILC. As high-achieving students on the pre-med track, they recognized that the stress and pressure experienced by pre-med students had become normalized. They wanted to research these challenges and provide resources and advice that could help students counter these factors and prioritize their mental well-being. While competing with their project, Zamam and Prathysha realized that their initiative could greatly benefit other students on campus. They decided to transform P.O.P. into a campus organization to provide greater resources and expand their outreach, allowing them to help as many students as possible. As Zamam and Prathysha always say, “Mistakes lead to mastery.”

Vice President

Fadeelah Usmani

Biological Sciences Pre-Med

Fadeelah Usmani is a junior majoring in Biological Sciences on the Pre-Medicine track. As a student pursuing a career in healthcare, she experiences academic pressure and stress on a daily basis. One of the main ways she manages her stress is by intentionally carving out time in her day for relaxing hobbies, including reading, painting, pottery, and henna. Working with her hands helps ground her and allows her to unwind after a long day. She also makes an effort to step back and acknowledge her hard work and accomplishments rather than overthinking what she has yet to complete or comparing herself to those around her. By regularly recognizing the progress she has made over time, Fadeelah is able to remain focused and motivated toward her goals while taking pride in her own journey. Fadeelah joined Progress Over Perfection (P.O.P.) because she recognizes that stress has unfortunately become a normalized part of the student experience for those pursuing careers in healthcare. Through P.O.P., she hopes to help challenge this standard and promote a healthier approach to navigating the pressures of the pre-health journey, reminding students that their well-being and progress matter just as much as their academic and professional achievements.

Treasurer

Bianca Giaccone

Biological Sciences Creative Writing Minor Pre-Med

Bianca Giaccone is a pre-med student majoring in Biological Sciences with a minor in Creative Writing. She joined Progress over Perfection (P.O.P.) aiming to find a community of people who have similar goals so everyone can work towards their dreams together. She hopes that other students who join P.O.P. can find this as well, so that they can lift each other up.
