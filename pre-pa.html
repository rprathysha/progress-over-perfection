Pre-PA: Preparing for a Career as a Physician Assistant

A look at what the Pre-PA path involves — and the student leading it within Progress Over Perfection.

What is Pre-PA?

Pre-PA is the pathway for students preparing for PA school and a career as a physician assistant — a collaborative, flexible role that allows practitioners to work across different medical specialties throughout their careers. The path centers on completing prerequisite coursework and accumulating patient care experience (PCE) hours before applying.

Between coursework, PCE hours, and applications, the pre-PA journey has its own set of pressures. Building sustainable habits early makes all the difference.

Prerequisite Coursework

A demanding sequence of science and health-related courses required for PA program applications.

Patient Care Experience

Hands-on PCE hours — often gained through certification programs or entry-level healthcare jobs — that PA schools require.

Pre-PA Chair

Breanna Graham

Charleston, South Carolina

Sophomore Biology Major Pre-PA Track

Breanna is a sophomore Biology major from Charleston, South Carolina, pursuing the Pre-Physician Assistant (Pre-PA) track. She has always wanted to pursue a career in medicine, even from a young age, but was never certain which specialty interested her most. Even after gaining experience with various medical specialties, she believes she would still want the opportunity to practice in different areas throughout her career. This flexibility, along with the collaborative nature of the physician assistant profession, are two of the many reasons that led her to pursue the PA path. Although she is still early in her pre-PA journey, Breanna identifies completing prerequisite courses and gaining patient care experience (PCE) hours as two of the most challenging aspects of the process. Rigorous coursework can be stressful, so she manages that stress by taking breaks when needed, working with others who are in the same courses, and making time each week for at least one activity unrelated to coursework. When it comes to gaining PCE hours, she encourages students to network with people they know to find certification programs or job opportunities that can provide the experience they need. Breanna joined Progress Over Perfection (P.O.P.) because she understands how difficult it can be to navigate life as a pre-health student while balancing challenging coursework and applications. Through her involvement in the organization, she hopes to share her experiences with others, continue working on her own mental well-being, and support her peers in prioritizing theirs. Ultimately, she hopes that students will find a sense of community within P.O.P. and feel more confident throughout their journeys as pre-health students.
