“Success isn't about being perfect — it's about making progress, embracing growth, and supporting one another.”

Progress Over Perfection is a student organization dedicated to supporting the mental health and well-being of students pursuing careers in healthcare. We believe that success isn’t about being perfect — it’s about making progress, embracing growth, and supporting one another through the challenges of our pre-health journey.

Through engaging events, honest conversations, wellness initiatives, peer support, and connections to campus and professional resources, we strive to reduce the stigma surrounding mental health while fostering a community where students feel empowered to prioritize both their ambitions and their well-being.
